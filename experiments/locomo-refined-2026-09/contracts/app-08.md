# Deborah and Jolene's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Deborah, Jolene, and independently evolving subjects.
The permitted first-session sample shows an electrical-engineering project, their late mothers,
meaningful homes and memorial objects, grief, yoga teaching, community support, and life goals.
These are initial signals, not a closed list.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Deborah and Jolene immediately. Create another person's page when named with
  a durable relationship, meaningful history/action, role, or repeated presence. A deceased family
  member can earn a page because their relationship and history remain independently useful.
  Record facts under the person they describe, not the person recounting them.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with the right person, history, traits, health,
  activities, and changes. A picture alone does not establish ownership.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, job/study path, goal, teaching/community practice,
  health or grief journey, hobby, travel plan, or other independently changing effort. Preserve
  beginnings, motives, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create only for a discrete event with several linked facts/stages, participants, or
  consequences; otherwise record it as a dated claim on the relevant person/thread.
- `places/`: create for a meaningful home, visit, move, recurring venue/activity, or plan. Record
  who, when, why, what happened there, and its significance.
- `objects/`: create for a named or repeatedly discussed pendant, photograph, keepsake, collection,
  vehicle, award, artwork, or artifact; record owner, giver/origin, date, supported appearance,
  symbolism, use, and changes without merging two people's similar objects.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, projects, dates, years, ages, counts, locations, relationships, preferences,
reasons, memories, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negatives. Preserve who
said what and distinguish empathy, analogy, intention, plan, and event. Similar experiences or
objects are not the same experience/object. Keep uncertainty. Omit greetings, generic sympathy,
and repetitions that add no factual or relational detail.

`[caption]` is a derived visual description; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids rather
than proof. Dialogue can identify a depicted person/object, but never merge identities or infer
details absent from both dialogue and caption.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative time to the session date, preserve the phrase, resolve exact days only when
unambiguous, and retain starts/endings. Jobs, roles, residence, ownership, project status,
deadlines, schedules, relationships, health/education state, and active preferences can change.
Use `supersede_claim` for changed state and `edit_claim` for an originally wrong claim. Historical
events such as a death or gift are not overwritten by later emotional change.

## Overview wording

People definitions state their relation to this conversation, not just occupation. Summaries
lead with current work, commitments, relationships, and important life threads. Places/objects
state whose they are and why they matter. Rewrite only on a changed picture and stay grounded.

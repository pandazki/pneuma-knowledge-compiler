# Design context for design skills

`DESIGN.md` (with its `DESIGN.zh-CN.md` mirror) is the design authority for this app; this
file only states, in the shape the design skills read, who the console is for and how it
should feel, so a skill run does not have to rediscover it. When the two disagree, `DESIGN.md`
wins and this file is wrong.

## Design Context

### Users
The Owner of a personal knowledge library, and the coding agent working as its Steward. The
Owner opens the console to check what the library holds and how it got there: a claim, its
source span, the compile round that wrote it, the job queue, a Steward session. Sessions are
short and deliberate; the Owner reads more than they type, and what they type is a question
to the library or a word to the Steward.

### Brand personality
Editorial, exact, quiet. The world is a galley proof on an editor's desk: paper, ink,
hairlines, footnotes. Every claim returns to an exact source span, and the interface should
feel like the place where that is checked — not like a dashboard, a chat app, or a control
panel.

### Aesthetic direction
Two independently tuned surfaces: light "Paper" (ink on paper) and dark "Lightbox" (film on a
lightbox). One accent, the blue pencil, reserved for links, selection, footnote numbers and
focus. Structure comes from typography, hairlines, margins and § numbering — never from
cards, glows or gradients. The ink of a state (ok, warn, danger) is a colour only where the
state is real. Anti-references: admin-dashboard chrome, glowing dark mode, glass, stripes.

### Design principles
1. Typography and hairlines carry the structure; containers are the exception.
2. A state is shown once, quietly, where it happens; nothing pulses for attention.
3. The blue pencil is rare: focus, selection and links, at the strength of a pencil mark on
   paper — a hairline inside the control and a faint tint of its ground, never a ring
   reaching outside the box.
4. Both surfaces are first-class; a decision is made twice, once per surface.
5. Copy is bilingual and earns its place; nothing restates what the reader can see.

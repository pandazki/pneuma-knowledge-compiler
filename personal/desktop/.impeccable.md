## Design Context

### Users
The Owner of a personal knowledge library, on their own Mac, glancing at the menu bar a few times a day: is the library up, did the last compile land, what is held, then a quick search, then a setting. Seconds, not sessions. Both Chinese and English text appear side by side (page titles, the Owner's own words, labels), so the type must carry CJK with the same care as Latin.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, bookish, exact. A library's reading-desk instrument, not a monitoring console. Three words: **elegant · library · dashboard**.

### Aesthetic Direction
The console's world is a galley proof — paper, ink, hairlines, footnotes, § numbering (apps/web/DESIGN.md). The tray is its pocket instrument: same paper/lightbox surfaces and tinted neutrals, same hairline-over-card rule, but built for readouts — tabular figures, dotted leaders like a book's table of contents, small-caps labels. Follows the system appearance (light "Paper" and dark "Lightbox" tuned separately, never inverted). Raycast is the interaction reference: instant, keyboard-first, a search field that is a first-class citizen. Anti-references: generic dark dashboards with glowing accents, card grids, big icons above headings, gradient text, side-stripe alerts.

### Design Principles
1. A readout is typography: a label, a leader, a number in tabular figures. No gauges, no sparklines, no cards.
2. Hairlines and space do the grouping. One accent (the console's blue pencil), rare.
3. Two real surfaces, each tuned; the panel is a piece of paper under a lamp or a slide on a lightbox.
4. Every word earns its place; Chinese and English are typeset as equals, never one as a fallback of the other.
5. Fast: opens with the last known state, keyboard reaches everything, motion is a 120 ms settle, never a bounce.

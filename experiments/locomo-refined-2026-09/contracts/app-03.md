# John and Maria's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of John, Maria, and independently evolving subjects. The
permitted first-session sample shows a family road trip, shelter volunteering, aerial yoga,
kickboxing, a local-politics goal, and education/infrastructure advocacy. These are starting
signals rather than a closed list.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create John and Maria immediately; create other people only when named with durable
  relationship, action, role, or repeated history. Record each person's relationships, work,
  education, preferences, skills, health, values, commitments, locations, possessions, and
  changes. Do not attach another person's fact to the listener.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal, tied to the right person, with history, traits,
  health, activities, and changes; an unnamed pictured animal does not establish ownership.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, campaign, goal, job/study path, exercise or hobby
  practice, volunteering, community issue, travel plan, or other independently evolving effort.
  Keep beginnings, reasons, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: use a page only for a discrete event with multiple connected facts, stages,
  participants, or later consequences; otherwise write a dated claim under its subject.
- `places/`: create when a visit, move, repeated activity, venue, or plan makes it independently
  useful; record who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for a named or repeatedly discussed possession, collection, vehicle, award,
  artwork, or artifact; record ownership, origin, supported appearance, uses, and changes.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations, preferences, reasons,
causal chains, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negative facts. Preserve
who asserted a fact and distinguish observation, proposal, plan, and result. Do not convert
praise or expectation into achievement. Keep uncertainty and disagreement. Omit only greetings,
generic encouragement, and repetitions adding no fact.

`[caption]` is a derived visual observation. `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids rather
than proof. Dialogue that identifies an image may be combined with its caption; neither licenses
details absent from both.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative time to the session occurrence date, retain the original phrase, resolve exact
days only when unambiguous, and preserve beginnings as well as endings. Jobs, public roles,
residence, ownership, campaign/project status, deadlines, scheduled plans, relationships,
health/education state, and active preferences can change. Use `supersede_claim` for a later
state and `edit_claim` only for a claim wrong when written. Intentions never become outcomes
without later evidence.

## Overview wording

People summaries lead with their current work, commitments, and life threads. Project/campaign
and event summaries lead with current status or outcome plus open conditions. Other subjects
define their connection to the people. Rewrite only on a changed picture and never exceed the
ledger's certainty.

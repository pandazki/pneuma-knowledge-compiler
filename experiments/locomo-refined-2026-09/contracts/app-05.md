# Tim and John's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Tim, John, and independently evolving subjects. The
permitted first-session sample shows a Harry Potter fan project, a new basketball team and role,
shooting goals, a season opener, London visits, and sneaker interests. These are initial signals,
not a closed list.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Tim and John immediately. Other people earn pages only when named with a
  durable relationship, role, action, or repeated history. Record the correct person's work,
  education, preferences, skills, health, values, commitments, relationships, locations,
  possessions, and changes.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with its person, history, traits, health, activities,
  and changes; never infer ownership from an unnamed picture.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, team/season, goal, job/study path, creative or
  athletic practice, collection, community activity, travel plan, or independently changing
  effort. Keep beginnings, reasons, participants, steps, challenges, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create for a discrete event only when several facts/stages, participants, or later
  consequences make it independent; otherwise use a dated subject claim.
- `places/`: create when a visit, move, repeated venue/activity, or plan makes the place useful;
  record who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for a named or repeated possession, jersey/sneaker collection, artwork,
  vehicle, award, or artifact; record ownership, origin, supported appearance, use, and change.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, team and project titles, positions, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations,
locations, preferences, reasons, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negatives.
Preserve attribution and distinguish discussion, proposal, plan, scheduled event, participation,
and achieved result. Keep uncertainty. Omit greetings, generic encouragement, and repetitions
that add no fact.

`[caption]` is a derived visual description; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids rather
than proof. Use dialogue to bind a pictured item/event to a participant, and never infer details
that neither dialogue nor caption supplies.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative time to the session date and keep the phrase; resolve an exact day only when
unambiguous. Record beginnings and endings. Teams, jobs, roles, residence, ownership, project or
season status, deadlines, scheduled plans, relationships, health/education state, and active
preferences can change. Use `supersede_claim` for a later true state and `edit_claim` only for an
original error. A future opener or collaboration remains future until confirmed.

## Overview wording

People summaries lead with current work, roles, commitments, and life threads. Project/team/event
summaries lead with current status/outcome and what remains open. Other subjects define their
connection to the participants. Rewrite only when the picture changes and stay within ledger
certainty.

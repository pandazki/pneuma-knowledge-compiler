# PKC personal desktop

**English** | [简体中文](PRODUCT.zh-CN.md)

PKC personal serves one person's knowledge libraries on one machine. The Owner brings
meetings, documents, messages and project sessions into a library whose compiled claims
remain linked to source evidence. The desktop tray provides a nearby reading and operating
surface: search, library health, sync controls and settings, with the browser console for
deeper reading and maintenance. The coding-agent Steward imports and maintains knowledge
through the framework's gated draft path. This is a knowledge library, not an agent memory
system.

The tray is quiet, bookish and exact: a reading-desk instrument for a quick glance or search.
Chinese and English deserve equal care. Known state, unknown state and failure must remain
distinguishable; presentation must not invent health or provenance. Visual rules belong in
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

Fold adds an experimental long-view retrospective. Knowledge arrives after events, so its
purpose is to connect continuing projects and themes, reflect on changes in direction, and
prepare questions for the next phase. Concrete events support that wider reading. Its six
forms offer source-date project involvement, current attention and free space, shaded
themes, saved-review mosaics, theme continuity and review-to-review redistribution.
The same engine-owned interface and per-library settings appear in tray and console.
Attention atlas is the initial preference; all six forms remain selectable. No form is
presented as universally best. The interface starts with controls, dates and the visual
content; cited reflection appears once below. Labels, counts, status and encoding keys
explain the content without slogans or a repeated introductory summary.

Two kinds of time remain distinct: the river places recorded involvement on original source
dates, while the time machine replays only reviews that were actually saved. Counts are not
work hours, missing observations are not inactivity, and earlier attention is not reconstructed.
Evidence and reasons remain inspectable. Owner notes provide context for a future review;
they neither execute tasks nor implicitly enter canonical knowledge.

Fold is an independent consumer of committed knowledge and dated source observations.
It does not alter ingestion, compilation or the canonical writer. Failures preserve the last
review. Saved reviews are retained judgments, not knowledge authority. Provenance checks
establish traceability, not semantic correctness; experimental judgment evaluations do not
establish production quality on the Owner's material.

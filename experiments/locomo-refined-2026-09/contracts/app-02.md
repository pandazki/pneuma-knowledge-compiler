# Jon and Gina's evolving personal history

Build a detailed, longitudinal record of Jon, Gina, and the independently evolving subjects in
their conversations. The permitted first-session sample shows job changes, a dance-studio goal,
dance styles, rehearsals, competitions, and a festival performance. These are starting signals,
not a closed list: later material decides what else exists.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Jon and Gina immediately. Create another person's page only when named with
  a durable relationship, action, role, or repeated history. Record relationships, work and
  education, preferences, skills, health, values, commitments, locations, possessions, and dated
  changes about that person; never misfile another person's fact because it was heard here.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with its person, history, traits, health, activities,
  and changes. An unnamed pictured animal is not automatically owned by a participant.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, business, goal, job/career path, study path,
  artistic or athletic practice, community activity, travel plan, or other independently changing
  effort. Keep beginnings, motives, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: a discrete event earns a page only with several connected facts, stages,
  participants, or later consequences. Otherwise use a dated claim on its person or thread.
- `places/`: create when a visit, move, repeated activity, venue, or plan makes the place useful.
  Record who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for a named or repeatedly discussed possession, artwork, collection, vehicle,
  award, or artifact. Record ownership, origin, supported appearance, dates, uses, and changes.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, titles, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations, preferences,
reasons, before/after states, important wording, and explicit negative facts. Preserve who
asserted each fact. A suggestion is not a plan, a plan is not an outcome, praise is not proof of
achievement, and a pictured example is not automatically the speaker's. Keep uncertainty and
disagreement. Omit greetings, filler, generic encouragement, and fact-free repetition.

`[caption]` is a derived visual description. `[query]` and `[image]` URL are retrieval/context
aids, not proof alone. When dialogue identifies the image, combine it with the caption without
inventing absent details or allowing the caption to override the speaker.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative time to the session date and retain the original expression. Resolve an exact
day only when unambiguous; otherwise keep an anchored span. Record starts as well as endings.
Jobs, roles, residence, ownership, business/project status, deadlines, scheduled plans,
relationships, health/education status, and active preferences are states. Use
`supersede_claim` when the world changes and `edit_claim` only for an originally wrong record.
Never silently promote an intention or scheduled performance to a completed one.

## Overview wording

Person definitions state their place in the conversation; summaries lead with current work,
commitments, and life threads. Thread/event summaries lead with current status or outcome and
open conditions. Other families define their connection to the people. Rewrite only when the
current picture changes and never state more certainty than the ledger.

# Joanna and Nate's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Joanna, Nate, and independently evolving subjects. The
permitted first-session sample shows writing, a first video-game tournament win, games, reading,
movie tastes, recommendations, and a valued physical copy. These are initial signals only.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Joanna and Nate immediately; create another person only when named with a
  durable relationship, action, role, or repeated history. Record work, education, preferences,
  skills, health, values, commitments, relationships, places, possessions, and dated changes
  about the correct person.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with owner/person, history, traits, health, activities,
  and changes. A pictured animal without explicit identification establishes no ownership.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, goal, work/study path, creative practice, hobby,
  competition path, community activity, travel plan, or other independently changing effort.
  Keep motives, beginnings, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create only for a discrete event with several linked facts, stages, participants,
  or later consequences; otherwise place a dated claim on the relevant person/thread.
- `places/`: create for a meaningful visit, move, repeated venue/activity, or plan and record
  who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for a named or repeatedly discussed possession, media copy, collection,
  award, artwork, vehicle, or artifact; record ownership, origin, supported appearance, use,
  dates, and changes.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names and titles, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations, preferences,
reasons, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negative facts. Preserve the
speaker and distinguish recommendations, intentions, scheduled actions, and outcomes. Praise or
expectation is not evidence of achievement. Retain uncertainty and disagreement. Omit greetings,
generic encouragement, and duplicate phrasing with no new fact.

`[caption]` is derived visual description; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids, not
standalone proof. Combine a speaker's explicit image identification with the caption, without
inventing details or letting the caption replace the account.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative expressions to the session date, retain their wording, and resolve exact days
only when unambiguous. Keep starts and ends. Jobs, residence, ownership, project/competition
status, deadlines, schedules, relationships, health/education state, and active preferences can
change. `supersede_claim` records a changed world; `edit_claim` fixes an originally wrong claim.
Never promote a recommendation or future intention into an event.

## Overview wording

People are defined by their place in this conversation and summarized by current work,
commitments, and life threads. Thread/event summaries state current status/outcome and open
conditions. Places/objects/companions define their human connection. Rewrite only when the
current picture changes, within the ledger's certainty.

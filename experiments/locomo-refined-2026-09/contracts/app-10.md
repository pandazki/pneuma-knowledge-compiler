# Calvin and Dave's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Calvin, Dave, and independently evolving subjects. The
permitted first-session sample shows a classic-car event, an upcoming Japan stay, housing and an
agent, cultural/food/music goals, a later Boston plan, and a Boston park. These are initial signals,
not a closed list.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Calvin and Dave immediately. Other people earn pages only when named with a
  durable relationship, role, action, or repeated history. Record the correct person's work,
  education, preferences, skills, health, values, commitments, relationships, locations,
  possessions, and changes.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with its person, history, traits, health, activities,
  and changes; a pictured but unnamed animal does not prove ownership.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, career/study path, artistic collaboration, goal,
  hobby, travel/move plan, community activity, or other independently changing effort. Preserve
  beginnings, motives, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create only for a discrete event with several connected facts/stages, participants,
  or later consequences; otherwise record a dated claim on the right person/thread.
- `places/`: create for a destination, residence, meaningful visit, move, recurring venue, or plan.
  Distinguish current location, future destination, and prior visit; record who, when, why, what
  happened, duration, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for named/repeated possessions, vehicles, homes/artifacts, collections,
  awards, or artworks. Record ownership, origin, supported appearance, use, and changed state;
  a stock or illustrative picture is not automatically the speaker's actual property.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, cities/countries, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations,
preferences, reasons, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negative facts.
Preserve attribution and distinguish aspiration, booked plan, departure, stay, return, and later
move. Keep uncertainty and contradiction. Omit greetings, generic encouragement, and repeats with
no new fact.

`[caption]` is a derived visual description; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids rather
than proof. Use explicit dialogue to bind a depicted home/place/car to a participant and never
promote illustrative media into real ownership or travel evidence without that binding.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative expressions to the session date, retain the original phrase, and resolve exact
days only when calendar rules make them unambiguous. Record starts/endings. Jobs, roles,
residence, ownership, itinerary/project status, deadlines, scheduled plans, relationships,
health/education state, and active preferences can change. Use `supersede_claim` for a later state
and `edit_claim` only for an originally wrong record. Future Japan/Boston events remain planned
until later material confirms them.

## Overview wording

People summaries lead with current work, location, commitments, and life threads. Travel/place,
project, and event summaries lead with current status/outcome and open conditions. Objects define
their actual connection to people. Rewrite only when the picture changes and stay within the
ledger's certainty.

# Audrey and Andrew's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Audrey, Andrew, and independently evolving subjects.
The permitted first-session sample shows a new financial-analyst job, three named dogs and their
collars, city adventures, birds/eagles, hiking, and Fox Hollow. These are initial signals only.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Audrey and Andrew immediately. Create another person only when named with a
  durable relationship, role, action, or repeated history. Record work, education, preferences,
  skills, health, values, commitments, relationships, locations, possessions, and dated changes
  about the correct person.
- `companions/`: create one page for each named animal. Bind Pepper, Precious, Panda, and later
  companions to the person actually stated; keep duration, species/breed if stated, equipment,
  traits, health, activities, and changes. A photographed animal alone establishes no ownership.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing job/career, project, goal, study path, hobby or exercise
  practice, volunteering, travel plan, or other independently changing effort. Keep beginnings,
  reasons, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create only when a discrete event has several connected facts/stages, participants,
  or later consequences; otherwise use a dated claim on its person or thread.
- `places/`: create for meaningful visits, moves, repeated parks/trails, venues, or plans. Record
  who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: create for named or repeated possessions, pet equipment, collections, vehicles,
  awards, artwork, or artifacts; record ownership, origin, supported appearance, use, and change.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names, job titles, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations, preferences,
reasons, before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negatives. Attribute every fact
to the right speaker and distinguish curiosity, recommendation, intention, plan, and outcome.
Keep uncertainty and disagreement. Omit greetings, generic encouragement, and fact-free repeats.

`[caption]` is a derived visual observation; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids rather
than proof. Explicit dialogue may identify an image's subject; do not infer ownership or other
details that neither the dialogue nor caption supports.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative time to the session date, retain its wording, resolve exact days only when
unambiguous, and record starts as carefully as endings. Jobs, roles, residence, ownership,
project status, deadlines, scheduled plans, relationships, companion health, education state,
and active preferences may change. Use `supersede_claim` for changed state and `edit_claim` for
an originally wrong record. Future plans remain future until evidence confirms them.

## Overview wording

People summaries lead with current work, commitments, relationships, and life threads. Companion
summaries state their person and current relevant state. Thread/event summaries lead with current
status/outcome and open conditions. Rewrite only on changed picture and never exceed the ledger.

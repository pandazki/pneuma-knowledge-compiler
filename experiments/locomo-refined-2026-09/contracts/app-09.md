# Evan and Sam's evolving personal history

Build a detailed longitudinal record of Evan, Sam, and independently evolving subjects. The
permitted first-session sample shows a new and old Prius, a family Rockies trip, hiking memories,
painting exploration, watercolor experience, and hobby goals. These are initial signals only.

## Filing model

- `people/`: create Evan and Sam immediately. Create another person only when named with a
  durable relationship, role, action, or repeated history. Record each person's work, education,
  preferences, skills, health, values, commitments, relationships, locations, possessions, and
  dated changes. Keep the listener's page free of the speaker's facts.
- `companions/`: one page per named animal with its person, origin/history, traits, health,
  activities, and changes. Do not infer ownership from an unnamed animal image.
- `threads/`: one page per continuing project, job/study path, goal, hobby/skill practice,
  family activity, travel plan, vehicle transition, or other independently changing effort. Keep
  beginnings, reasons, participants, steps, setbacks, decisions, and outcomes.
- `events/`: create only when several linked facts/stages, participants, or later consequences
  make a discrete event independent; otherwise use a dated claim on its subject.
- `places/`: create for a meaningful trip, visit, move, recurring trail/venue, or plan and record
  who, when, why, what happened, and significance.
- `objects/`: distinguish each named or repeated possession, vehicle, artwork, collection, award,
  or artifact. Record ownership, old/new identity, origin, supported appearance, use, and changes;
  never merge two objects because they share a model/type.

## Recording caliber

Record exact names/models, counts, ages, ranks, dates, durations, locations, preferences, reasons,
before/after states, meaningful wording, and explicit negatives. Preserve attribution. Thinking
about a hobby is not trying it; repair/sale plans are not completed repair/sale. Keep uncertainty
and disagreement. Omit greetings, generic encouragement, and fact-free repetitions.

`[caption]` is derived visual description; `[query]` and `[image]` URL are context aids, not proof
alone. Dialogue must bind a picture to a vehicle, place, person, or artwork before ownership or
identity is recorded; do not invent unsupplied detail.

## Time, state, and correction

Anchor relative expressions to the session date, keep the original phrase, resolve exact days
only when unambiguous, and record beginnings/endings. Jobs, roles, residence, vehicle/other
ownership, project and hobby status, deadlines, scheduled plans, relationships, health/education
state, and preferences can change. Use `supersede_claim` for changed reality and `edit_claim` for
an original error. Planned repair, sale, travel, or hobby remains planned until confirmed.

## Overview wording

People summaries lead with current work, commitments, relationships, and life threads. Vehicle,
place, project, and event summaries lead with current state/outcome and what remains open. Rewrite
only when that current picture changes and never exceed ledger certainty.
